Research based approach

From biology to banking.

A published method for measuring how one observation deviates from the relationships expected in a reference population. It was introduced in biology, generalised to any time independent scalar data, and reached financial transactions in 2018.

One observation One network

The difference

Published work, 2018

network  →  features  →  model  →  alert

The model raises the case. What raised it is a score.

Deviare

network  →  rule  →  alert  →  model removes  →  human decides

The network is the rule that raises it, and the model may only take away.

Proprietary

The representation is published. Parenclitic Deterministic Decision Making with Explainable AI (xAI) is not: the feature set, the reference population, the decision logic that turns network structure into a rule a supervisor can read, the thresholds, and the two stage architecture that leaves the model no authority to raise a case. That is what Deviare owns, and it is what a replay on your own data tests.

Where the method has been tested

2011

Obstructive nephropathy. The parenclitic representation is introduced.

Zanin & Boccaletti, Chaos 21:033103

2014

Arabidopsis thaliana. 15 previously unknown key genes, confirmed in vivo.

Zanin et al., Sci Rep 4:5112

2017

DNA methylation, 10 cancers. 15,295 variables reduced to 12 topology measures, 93 to 99% accuracy.

Karsakov et al., PLoS ONE 12:e0169661

2018

Credit card fraud. The method reaches financial transactions.

Zanin et al., Complexity 2018:5764370

2025

Sepsis, intensive care. Patient level deviations predict 30 day mortality.

Ito et al., Physiol Rep 13:e70407

Every figure above is taken from the published abstract. Sample sizes and the conditions attached to each result are stated in the papers, because that is the first thing a reviewer checks.

On scope: the 2014 paper describes the representation as applying to “time independent, scalar data sets”, not to biological data alone. Introduced in Zanin & Boccaletti, Chaos 21:033103 (2011) and generalised in Zanin et al., Sci Rep 4:5112 (2014).
