01 / The method

Rules raise. The model only removes.

Two stages with unequal authority. That asymmetry is the product, because it is what lets a supervisor be told why a case exists without being told to trust a score.

Stage one  ·  raises

Parenclitic rules raise every alert

Every transaction is mapped as a network of deviations from legitimate behaviour. The structure of that network is the alert, and the rule that fired is the explanation.

Stage two  ·  only removes

The model removes the noise

The classifier removes false positives from the alerts the rules raised. It never adds an alert. Strict AND, and the threshold is a risk lever your own team sets.

Proprietary

The underlying representation is published and peer reviewed. What is ours is Parenclitic Deterministic Decision Making with Explainable AI (xAI): the feature set, the reference population, the decision logic that turns network structure into a rule a supervisor can read, the thresholds, and the two stage architecture that leaves the model no authority to raise a case.

04 / Governance

Governed by construction. Proven in eight weeks.

It runs inside your account. It does not touch your monitoring system, your operations partner, your investigation team or your data.

Why now

2027

AMLR applies from 10 July 2027. It entered into force on 9 July 2024.

Regulation (EU) 2024/1624

2026

Independent testing of policies, procedures and controls is required under the Danish AML Act from 15 September 2026 (hvidvaskloven § 8, Lov nr. 764 of 8 September 2026). A replay against a versioned policy rebuilds how the control behaved on any past day. That is the record such a test rests on.

Enforced contracts

A wrong value stops the build

Every table’s shape is enforced before it exists. A wrong type or a missing value fails the build instead of reaching an investigator. It never reaches a case file.

Named approval

A person signs the model

No model goes live without a person’s name on it. Every change is reviewed and signed, and lineage is generated from the running system rather than maintained beside it.

Replay

Any past state rebuilds

Any past alert re-runs against the policy that was in force that day. A finding you cannot rebuild is a finding you cannot defend in front of a supervisor.
